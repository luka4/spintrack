What you can do with SpinBook

  • Training & match diary

    Log training sessions and friendly, league and tournament matches with scores, opponents, venues and photos.

  • Rubber wear tracking

    See the estimated health, speed, spin and control of every rubber, based on hours played and its durability rating.

  • Equipment inventory

    Keep your rubbers, blades and complete rackets in one place, picked from a built-in catalog.

  • Friends & shared matches

    Add friends by username — matches you play together can be imported straight into their diary.

  • Stats & progress

    Follow your win/loss record and playing time, earn XP, level up and keep your daily streak going.

  • Works without an account

    Everything stays on your phone. Sign in with Google or Apple for cloud backup and sync across devices.

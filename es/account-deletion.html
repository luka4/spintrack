SpinBook - Account & Data Deletion Request

SpinBook (by luka4) lets you request deletion of your account and associated cloud data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@getspinbook.com
  2. Use the same email address that you use to sign in to SpinBook (e.g.: the Google account email).
  3. Use the subject: SpinBook — Delete my account
  4. In the email body, include:

- Your SpinBook username - The Google account email address used in the app - A clear statement that you want your account deleted

Example:

Subject: SpinBook — Delete my account

Hello, Please delete my SpinBook account and associated data. Username: peter22 Account email: peter22@example.com

Thank you.

What will be deleted

When your request is verified, we delete the cloud data associated with your SpinBook account, which may include:

  • Your profile (username, display name, avatar)
  • Your friends / social connections and inbox items
  • Your synced diary entries (training/matches), notes, and related metadata
  • Your synced inventory (rubbers, blades, rackets)
  • Any photos you uploaded for activities and your profile avatar (stored in cloud storage)

What may be kept

  • Emails you send to support may be retained for support/legal purposes.

Important note about shared activities

If you shared an activity with a friend, they receive a separate copy of that activity. When your account is deleted, your friend’s copy remains in their account and is not deleted automatically.

Retention period

Deletion requests are typically processed within [10 business days]. If a longer retention period is required (for legal/security reasons), we will inform you by email.

Important note

If you used SpinBook in guest/offline mode only (no sign-in), your data is stored on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the app.
